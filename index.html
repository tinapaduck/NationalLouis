01 / ABOUT

More than a job title.

I approach technology with a simple question: How can this work better? I'm drawn to the systems, processes, and people behind meaningful change—from enterprise platforms and business operations to the everyday challenges that benefit from clearer thinking.

My interests include enterprise applications, digital transformation, operational improvement, and the relationship between technical decisions and business outcomes. I value practical solutions, open communication, and continuous learning.

This site is a place to share the professional and personal interests that shape my perspective.
